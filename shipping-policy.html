Shipping Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Shipping Charges

  • Free shipping on all orders above ₹999.
  • A flat ₹49 shipping charge applies to orders of ₹999 or less.

Processing Time

Orders are processed within 1–2 working days after payment confirmation. During festive seasons, processing may take slightly longer.

Delivery Time

Delivery usually takes 3–7 working days depending on your location. Remote areas may take a little longer.

How Your Order Is Delivered

Orders are shipped through trusted courier partners. Once shipped, you will receive a tracking number, and you can follow your order on our Track Order page.

Order Tracking

Enter your order number on the Track Order page any time to see the latest status of your order.

Incorrect Address

Please double-check your address and phone number at checkout. Any reshipment or extra courier charges due to incorrect address details will be borne by the customer.

Lost or Damaged Parcels

If your parcel is lost or arrives damaged, contact us within 48 hours on WhatsApp +91 81254 91097 and we will resolve it promptly.
