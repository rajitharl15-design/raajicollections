Our Story

Considered clothing, made to be worn, loved and kept.

Peacock Fashions began with a simple idea — that great fashion should be easy to find, fairly made and genuinely loved. Today we bring together a curated edit of women's, men's, kids' and accessories — designed for real life.

We work with natural, traceable materials and responsible factories, because we believe lasting style never has to cost the planet.
