# Instagram — Super Simple 3-Step Guide

## Step 1 — Make your account (5 min, once)

- Open Instagram app → Sign up
- Username: use your shop name, e.g. `raaji.collections`
- Go to Profile → Edit Profile → paste in **Bio**:
  `Raaji Collections | Traditional women's wear | Shop online 👇`
- Tap **Add link** → paste your site:
  `https://rajitharl15-design.github.io/raajicollections/`
- Done. Your profile now has a working "Shop Now" button.

---

## Step 2 — Post 1 photo every day (3 min)

- Tap **+** → choose a product photo (same one as on your website)
- Tap Next → Next
- Paste this caption (change the product name):

  ✨ Our collection! ✨
  Beautiful sarees, blouses, dresses & jewellery.
  🛍️ Shop online — link in bio
  ✅ Free shipping above ₹999
  #SareeOnline #IndianFashion #HyderabadFashion

- Tap **Share**

---

## Step 3 — When someone messages you

- Reply: "Please order on our website — link in bio 🙏"
- Orders + payment happen ONLY on your website + WhatsApp.
- Never take orders or payment in Instagram DMs.

---

### The whole idea
Post photo every day → people tap your bio link → they visit your site → they buy.

That's it. Do Step 1 once, then Step 2 daily.
