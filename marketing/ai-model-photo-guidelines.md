# Raaji Collections — AI Model Photo Guidelines

Your products are real; the AI only changes the model/person wearing them.
This keeps online orders trustworthy (fewer returns, no disputes, better growth).

## Golden Rule
The AI can change the PERSON — never the PRODUCT.
Product colour, pattern, weave, print and fit must match what you will actually ship.

---

## Checklist for EVERY product photo

Before uploading a photo to Admin/Instagram, confirm:

- [ ] Product colour = real product colour (no boosting/change)
- [ ] Pattern/print/design = exactly the real product
- [ ] Fabric texture look is honest (not "more premium" than reality)
- [ ] No defects hidden (match the real item's quality)
- [ ] Price on site matches the photo's look/finish
- [ ] Same product also has 1 REAL photo (flat lay or hanging) so buyers see the true fabric
- [ ] Caption/description notes the material honestly (silk/cotton/georgette etc.)

---

## What the AI model may safely do

- Change the model's dress/body to show size & drape
- Use neutral backgrounds / festive backdrops
- Show colour-coordination ideas (styling)
- Present multiple angles

## What the AI model must NOT do

- Change the product's colours/pattern/print
- Add shine/finish the real fabric doesn't have
- Hide or fix defects
- Change length/details (blouse cut, saree border, sequence work)

---

## Disclaimer text (add to product description)

"Actual product colours may slightly vary from photos due to screen settings
and lighting. Refer to the fabric/material details before ordering."

## Return-avoidance tips

1. For sarees/blouses: state exact length, size, material in description.
2. Keep 1 real photo alongside 1 AI-model photo.
3. Use consistent sizing in AI images (don't show slim-fit if it's a loose cut).
4. Photograph 2 items per day with the checklist → build a trustworthy catalog.

---

## Weekly habit

Every Friday: spot-check 3 products on the site against your checklist.
Fix/retake anything that looks different from the real item.
