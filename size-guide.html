How to Measure

  • Bust/Chest: measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape comfortable.
  • Waist: measure around your natural waistline.
  • Shoulder: measure from one shoulder point to the other across the back.
  • If you are between sizes, we recommend the larger size.
