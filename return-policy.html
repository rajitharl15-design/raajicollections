Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Our Promise

We want you to love your purchase. If something isn’t right, raise a request within 7 days of delivery and we’ll help resolve it — via return, exchange or store credit.

When Can You Return / Exchange?

  • Wrong item delivered — exchange or full refund, no questions asked.
  • Defective / damaged item — replacement or full refund.
  • Size not suitable (sarees, blouses, dresses, kids wear) — exchange for the correct size or store credit, subject to stock availability.
  • Changed your mind — exchange or store credit, if the item is unused and in original condition.

Conditions for Return

  • The item must be unused, unwashed, unworn and in its original packaging.
  • Tags and labels must be intact.
  • Report any damage within 48 hours of delivery with a photo/video on WhatsApp.
  • Custom / stitched-to-order garments are not returnable.

How to Raise a Return

  1. Message us on WhatsApp +91 81254 91097 with your order number.
  2. Share a photo/video clearly showing the issue.
  3. We’ll confirm eligibility and give you the return instructions.
  4. Pack the item securely and send it back to our address.

Refunds

  • Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 working days after we receive the returned item.
  • Refunds are made via UPI / bank transfer to the same number used for payment.
  • Return shipping is not refunded unless the return is due to our error (wrong/damaged item).

Exchanges

If the correct size or colour is in stock, we’ll send a free replacement. If not, we’ll issue store credit or a refund.
