Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

At Raaji Collections, we respect your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website or place an order, and how we use and protect it.

Information We Collect

  • Contact details you provide at checkout: name, phone number, address, city, state and PIN code.
  • Order details: items ordered, order number and payment confirmation code.
  • If you subscribe to our newsletter, we store your email address.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process, prepare and deliver your orders.
  • To confirm payment and communicate order updates on WhatsApp.
  • To send updates, offers and discounts (only if you subscribed).
  • To improve our products and services.

What We Do NOT Do

  • We never sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties.
  • We do not store payment card numbers. Payments are arranged manually via WhatsApp / UPI.

Data Storage & Security

Your details are stored in a secure database and are accessible only to the store owner for order fulfilment. We take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Your Rights

You may ask us to view, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by contacting us on WhatsApp or email.
